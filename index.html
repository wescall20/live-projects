Meet your guide

Meet Dr. Laurie Callihan

Laurie is an educator, homeschool parent and experienced guide for families who need more than another list of resources.

Her work brings together more than 20 years of public-school teaching, homeschooling five children, special-needs expertise, curriculum guidance, evaluations, testing and high-school opportunity planning.

She helps parents understand the learner entrusted to them and make sound educational decisions without turning the home into another institutional classroom.

Ph.D. in Science Education · Florida-certified teacher · ESE, K–6, Biology and Middle School Science · Homeschool parent of five · Author and national speaker
